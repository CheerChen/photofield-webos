# Lofi loops

The kiosk color keys (red / green / yellow / blue) each select a playlist of
7 lofi tracks. Each playlist is shuffled without repeats; after it finishes,
Kiosk chooses another color at random. Up/Down step through the active color's
playlist.

- **Red — morning lights:** Temple at Dawn, Bells Before Sunrise, Paper Lantern
  Rain, Lanterns in Slow Motion, Fireplace Loop, Embers After Midnight, Dusk on
  Red Earth.
- **Green — bamboo and tea mist:** Bamboo Shadow Waltz, Moon Through Bamboo,
  Teacup Morning Fog, Misty Steam Quiet Dreams, Mist Over Green Fields, Spring
  Garden Loops, A Taste of Spring.
- **Yellow — golden hour:** Soft Gold Sky, Porchlight Golden Hour, Window Seat
  Daydream, Dust on the Morning Keys, Sunset Offbeat, Glow on the Overpass, Dusk
  Between Stoops.
- **Blue — night and floating ambience:** Moonlit Moss, Drifting Through Fog,
  Soft Weightless Hours, Warm Constellations, Blue Below the Surface, Sea Glass
  Evening, Satellite Lullaby.

Tracks are **CC0 1.0 (public domain)** from the **Open Lo-Fi** collection by
btahir/open-lofi, bundled locally in this directory.

- Source: https://github.com/btahir/open-lofi
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

`scripts/generate_lofi.py` is the earlier deterministic synth generator; its
output is no longer used but is kept for reference.
